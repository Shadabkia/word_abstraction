# Pars Ra Pas — Platform Vision

## Platform Overview

A narrative-driven platform of Persian word games inside a fictional social app.

Pars Ra Pas is not a single game — it's a platform of many small, beautifully designed word and puzzle games. What makes it unique is that everything happens inside a fictional Instagram-like app that belongs to the main character, Kian.

The result is a game that blends:
- A rich comedic story
- A scrolling social feed full of NPC posts
- Multiple puzzle games and modes
- A character-driven journey across Iran
- A cozy, tactile, Instagram-inspired UI

Every part of the app — Feed, Messages, Profile, Arcade, Dashboard — forms a living, narrative ecosystem through which the player explores Kian’s life and plays dozens of different word-based challenges.

## 1. Core Concept

At its heart, Pars Ra Pas is:
**A narrative adventure told through a social network interface, where every memory you unlock is a puzzle you must solve.**

Kian escapes a suffocating office life and drives across Iran in a van called The Onion. As he travels, the player unlocks:
- Levels → Moments of his journey
- Posts → NPC perspectives
- Messages → Quests, hints, and humor
- Mini-games → Various word-based mechanics
- Chapters → New cities, new culture, new characters

This is not a linear story — it’s a social-media-shaped narrative world.

## 2. Platform of Games

Pars Ra Pas includes multiple puzzle and word game systems, not just one.

Examples (not exhaustive):
- Word Association (4-row matching)
- Word Search inside a grid
- Guessing games
- Semantic puzzles
- Wordscape-like explorations
- Timed modes
- Endless modes
- Daily challenges

The game is modular, allowing new modes to be added easily.
This structure makes it feel more like: **A pocket arcade inside a storybook.**

## 3. The Five Tabs — The Player’s World

The UI is built around a familiar 5-tab navigation bar, just like Instagram, but translated into a warm, handcrafted Persian aesthetic.

### 1. Feed Tab — The Lore Stream
- A vertical Instagram-style feed of NPC posts, images, captions, and comments.
- Daily-updating, evolving with player progress.
- Stories row = Daily Challenges.

### 2. Arcade Tab — The Game Library
- A clean list of all available mini-games.
- Tap any game → See its levels → Play instantly.
- No search, no categories. Simple and delightful.

### 3. Dashboard / Home Tab — The Heart
- The central tab with a big Van icon.
- Shows:
    - Hero Card → Continue Campaign
    - Quick access to game modes
    - Smart recommendations (time-of-day, session count, progress)
    - Coins counter, Vibes (energy) meter, and world notifications
- This is where the journey begins and continues.

### 4. Messages Tab — Quests & Story Beats
- Read-only DMs from NPCs.
- New quests, new characters, hints, and comedic story moments.
- Organized like Instagram DMs with unread highlights.

### 5. Profile Tab — Campaign Mode & Progress
- A full Instagram-style profile page.
- Chapters = Story Highlights.
- Levels = Posts in a 3×3 grid.
- Tap a level → Instagram-style detail → Play.
- Avatar, bio, and follower count evolve as Kian’s life evolves.

## 4. Narrative Experience

The story is whimsical, Dahl-esque, humorous, and deeply Iranian.

Kian’s journey unfolds through:
- Posts
- Captions
- NPC comments
- DMs
- Puzzle content
- City-based chapters

Every level’s content is a fragment of his life — a memory, a moment, a challenge — wrapped around a mini-game mechanic.
You unlock the story by playing games and “posting” the results.

## 5. Emotional & Aesthetic Goals

Pars Ra Pas aims to feel:
- Warm
- Familiar (Instagram)
- Locally authentic
- Funny and human
- Tactile, juicy, and craft-like
- Full of Persian cultural flavor without being heavy

It is a digital road trip, a diary, a comedy, a puzzle platform, and a social media parody all at once.

## 6. The Player’s Loop

1. Open Dashboard
2. Continue campaign or try a mode
3. Complete level → reveals memory
4. Memory turns into a post
5. NPCs react in Feed
6. Messages deliver new quests
7. More levels unlock
8. Player explores more mini-games in Arcade

This creates an ongoing rhythm:
**Play → Post → Explore → Read → Unlock → Repeat**

## 7. The Magic of the Design

The magic of Pars Ra Pas is that UI itself is the world.

There is no separation between:
- Storytelling
- Gameplay
- Navigation
- Character interactions

Everything happens inside a single “fictional social app” that the player grows to understand as Kian’s inner world.

## Final Essence

Pars Ra Pas is a platform of playful Persian word games wrapped inside a narrative social-media interface. By browsing NPC posts, reading DMs, playing mini-games, and scrolling through Kian’s evolving profile, the player experiences a comedic, emotional road trip across Iran — one post, one memory, and one puzzle at a time.
