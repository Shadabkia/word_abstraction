# Business Plan — Pars Ra Pas

## Overview

Pars Ra Pas operates as a free-to-play (F2P) mobile game with in-app purchases (IAP) as the primary revenue model. The business strategy prioritizes user acquisition, engagement, and long-term retention through narrative-driven content and cultural authenticity, while maintaining a clean, ad-free experience in early phases.

## Core Business Model

**Free-to-Play with In-App Purchases**
- Game is free to download and play
- Core content and basic gameplay available without payment
- Premium features and enhancements available through IAP
- Revenue generated through optional purchases that enhance the experience

## Current Phase Strategy (Pre-100K MAU)

### No Advertising
- **Zero Ads**: Maintain ad-free experience to preserve narrative immersion and cultural authenticity
- **Focus on Organic Growth**: Build user base through word-of-mouth, social sharing, and content quality
- **Clean User Experience**: Prioritize the Instagram-like UI and Persian storytelling without interruptions

### Revenue Streams (IAP-Only)

#### 1. Premium Currency: Vibe s
**Purpose**: Accelerate progress and enhance personalization without breaking core gameplay

**Purchase Options**:
- Small packs (100 Vibes: $0.99)
- Medium packs (500 Vibes: $4.99)
- Large packs (1200 Vibes + bonus: $9.99)
- Mega packs (2500 Vibes + bonus: $19.99)

**Vibes Usage**:
- Skip challenging puzzles
- Unlock bonus story content
- Purchase cosmetic upgrades
- Access premium mini-games

#### 2. Energy System: Fuel
**Purpose**: Create natural engagement rhythm while providing monetization opportunities

**Mechanics**:
- Limited daily fuel for campaign levels (3-5 attempts)
- Free regeneration over time (30 minutes per fuel)
- IAP options for instant refills

**Purchase Options**:
- Single refill (1 Fuel: $0.49)
- Pack of 3 refills ($1.99)
- Unlimited fuel for 24 hours ($2.99)

#### 3. Cosmetic Personalization
**Purpose**: Allow players to express cultural identity and journey progression

**Categories**:
- **Van Customization**: Paint jobs, stickers, and accessories for "The Onion"
- **Character Outfits**: Kian's clothing reflecting story stages
- **Profile Themes**: Persian cultural motifs and special borders
- **Achievement Cosmetics**: Premium badge designs and animations

#### 4. Content Expansion Packs
**Purpose**: Extend the narrative experience with optional story branches

**Types**:
- **Director's Cut Chapters**: Enhanced story content with exclusive memories
- **Alternate Endings**: "What if" storylines exploring different paths
- **Cultural Deep Dives**: Expanded Persian cultural content and locations

## Future Phase: Advertising Integration (Post-100K MAU)

### Advertising Triggers
- **MAU Threshold**: Activate when consistently reaching 100,000+ monthly active users
- **Revenue per MAU**: Current IAP revenue supports user base growth without ads
- **Quality Assurance**: Ads only introduced when core experience is proven and user base is stable

### Planned Ad Integration
**Non-Intrusive Ad Types**:
- **Rewarded Video Ads**: Optional ads for bonus fuel/vibes
- **Banner Ads**: Subtle placements in social feed areas
- **Interstitial Ads**: Between major story transitions (skippable)

**Ad Revenue Goals**:
- Supplement IAP revenue (target 15-25% of total revenue)
- Never compromise narrative flow or cultural authenticity
- User opt-out options for premium subscribers

## Market Strategy

### Primary Markets
1. **Persian-Speaking Regions**: Iran, Iranian diaspora (US, Canada, Europe, UAE)
2. **Global Puzzle Gaming**: English-speaking markets interested in cultural games
3. **Educational Segment**: Persian language learners and cultural enthusiasts

### User Acquisition
- **Organic Growth**: Social sharing through Instagram-like UI
- **Cultural Marketing**: Partner with Persian influencers and media
- **Cross-Promotion**: Collaborate with Persian gaming communities
- **App Store Optimization**: Target Persian language keywords and cultural themes

### Live Operations
- **Regular Content Updates**: New chapters, mini-games, and events
- **Seasonal Events**: Persian holiday-themed challenges (Norouz, etc.)
- **Community Engagement**: Social features and player-generated content opportunities

## Financial Projections

### Revenue Mix (Initial Phase)
- **100% IAP**: Focus on high-quality purchases that enhance experience
- **No Ad Revenue**: Preserve clean experience for user acquisition

### Key Metrics to Track
- **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**: Target $2-5 in first 6 months
- **Conversion Rate**: 5-10% of players making purchases
- **Retention Rates**:
  - Day 1: >40%
  - Day 7: >20%
  - Day 30: >10%
- **Session Length**: 15-25 minutes

### Growth Milestones
- **Phase 1 (Launch-10K MAU)**: Establish core mechanics and narrative
- **Phase 2 (10K-50K MAU)**: Optimize IAP offerings and user experience
- **Phase 3 (50K-100K MAU)**: Scale content production and community features
- **Phase 4 (100K+ MAU)**: Introduce optional advertising features

## Competitive Advantages

### Cultural Authenticity
- Authentic Persian storytelling and cultural representation
- Local language support and regional relevance
- Educational value through language and culture integration

### Technical Excellence
- Modular content system enabling rapid iteration
- Cross-platform compatibility (iOS/Android)
- Scalable architecture for global expansion

### User Experience Focus
- Ad-free initial experience prioritizes quality over quantity
- Narrative-driven engagement creates emotional investment
- Instagram-like UI encourages social sharing and community building

## Risk Mitigation

### Market Risks
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Regular consultation with Persian cultural experts
- **Regional Access**: VPN-friendly design for restricted markets
- **Competition**: Differentiate through unique cultural storytelling

### Technical Risks
- **Content Scalability**: JSON/DSL system designed for rapid expansion
- **Platform Changes**: Cross-platform architecture reduces dependency
- **Performance**: Mobile-first design ensures broad device compatibility

### Business Risks
- **IAP Dependency**: High-quality offerings ensure player willingness to pay
- **User Acquisition**: Cultural authenticity drives organic growth
- **Monetization Balance**: Respectful IAP design maintains player trust

## Success Definition

**Short-term (6 months)**: 10,000+ MAU, positive user feedback, sustainable IAP revenue
**Medium-term (12 months)**: 50,000+ MAU, established community, profitable operations
**Long-term (24 months)**: 100,000+ MAU, global recognition, diversified revenue streams

## Final Principles

1. **Culture First**: Business decisions enhance, never compromise, Persian cultural representation
2. **Quality Over Quantity**: Prefer engaged, paying users over mass ad-supported audience
3. **Sustainable Growth**: Build lasting relationships through authentic storytelling and fair monetization
4. **Iterative Learning**: Use data and user feedback to refine business model continuously

This business plan positions Pars Ra Pas as a culturally authentic, financially sustainable game that respects its Persian heritage while building a global audience through compelling narrative-driven gameplay.
